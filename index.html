A note from Emily

“My daughter is Fin. Nothing inspires a children’s book quite like watching your three‑year‑old choose a screen over kids asking her to play...”

Emily Chen spent her career in tech and product design—so she knows a thing or two about how the sausage is made in today’s addictive digital world. She has always loved art, design, and doodling. Look Up, Fin! is her first children’s book, after having read hundreds of books to her own kids. She lives in Pasadena, California by way of New York City and Boston.
